Ultimate Car Mod
How to Build a Car

Before you can drive anywhere you need to build a car. You don't make it in a regular crafting table — you need a special Car Workshop. Here's how:

  1. 1 Build the Car Workshop. Craft one Car Workshop block and eight Car Workshop parts. Place the main block down, then put the eight parts around it (like a 3×3 square with the main block in the middle). Right-click the middle block to open it.
  2. 2 Pick a body. Choose what your car looks like. Wooden bodies are the easiest to make and come in every wood type. There are also bigger options: Big Wooden Body, Transporter (like a van), SUV, and Sportscar. Non-wooden bodies can be dyed any Minecraft colour.
  3. 3 Add wheels. Normal cars need 4 small wheels. If you picked a Transporter you'll need 6 wheels. The SUV needs 4 big wheels. Each wheel goes in its own slot in the workshop.
  4. 4 Choose an engine. There are three options: 3-cylinder (slower, uses less fuel), 6-cylinder (faster, uses more fuel), or truck engine (the most fuel-efficient — great for long trips).
  5. 5 Pick a fuel tank. Small tank holds half a bucket of fuel, medium holds 1 bucket, large holds 1.5 buckets. A bigger tank means fewer trips to the fuel station.
  6. 6 Build it! Put all the parts in the workshop and craft your car. It will appear right there — hop in!
Extras You can also add a license plate holder (so you can write a custom name on your car), a bumper (wooden cars only, just for looks), or a container on a Transporter to carry lots of items or fluids.
Repairs If your car gets damaged, drive it over the workshop and use repair kits in the workshop GUI. Each kit fixes 5% of the damage.

How to Drive Your Car

Once your car is built and has some fuel in it, here's everything you need to know to drive it.

  1. 1 Get in. Right-click the car to sit in it.
  2. 2 Start the engine. Hold down R until the engine starts. If it's cold (you haven't driven it in a while) it takes a little longer. A damaged car also takes longer to start.
  3. 3 Drive! Use your normal movement keys to go forwards, backwards, and steer. Press I while inside to open the car screen and check your fuel level and damage.
  4. 4 Refuel. Drive up to a fuel station and right-click it with a canister to refuel. Or shift-right-click the fuel station. If you completely run out of fuel, you can slowly push the car by sneaking (crouching) and walking into it.
  5. 5 Lock your car. Every car comes with 2 keys. Right-click a key while standing within 25 blocks of your car to lock or unlock it. You can make a spare key by combining a key with an iron ingot in the crafting table.
Watch out Cars take damage from crashing into blocks or other vehicles, and from driving through water or lava. Check your damage bar in the car screen (press I) and repair before it gets too bad.
Breaking down a car To pick up a car and remove it, you need to hit it twice quickly (within half a second) while holding a hammer, screwdriver, or wrench.

How to Build Roads

Most cars can only drive up a half-block step at a time — so if your road goes up or down a hill you need to use slopes, otherwise your car will get stuck. Here's how to build good roads:

  1. 1 Use Asphalt blocks for flat roads. Craft asphalt blocks and lay them down as your road surface. They look great and cars drive smoothly on them.
  2. 2 Use Asphalt Slopes for hills. Wherever your road goes up or down, replace the step with an asphalt slope or asphalt slab. This creates a gentle ramp your car can actually drive up.
  3. 3 Add road markings with Painters. Craft a painter and right-click it on your asphalt to add lane markings (white lines, yellow lines, etc.). Sneak and right-click to change the marking style.
  4. 4 Add Guardrails on the edges. Place guardrails along the sides of your road to stop cars from accidentally driving off the edge.
  5. 5 Add Signs. Place signs and right-click them to add text — up to 4 lines per side. Useful for labelling roads or pointing players in the right direction.
Important The SUV is the only car that can handle normal block-height steps. Every other car needs slopes on hills — don't skip them or your car will just sit there bumping against the wall!

How to Make Fuel for Your Car

Cars in this mod run on biodiesel — a special fuel you make yourself from plants and wood. It takes a few steps but once you have it set up it keeps making fuel automatically. Here's how to do it:

  1. 1 Grow Canola. Break some tall grass to find canola seeds — they drop just like wheat seeds. Plant them on farmland near water and wait for them to grow fully before harvesting.
  2. 2 Build an Oil Mill and a Dynamo. Craft an oil mill and place it down. Then craft a dynamo with a crank and connect it to the mill with a cable. The dynamo is like a hand-powered battery — you right-click the crank to spin it and give the mill power.
  3. 3 Squeeze the canola into oil. Put your canola into the oil mill and power it with the dynamo. It will press out canola oil (a liquid). You'll also get canola cake as a leftover — you can feed it to animals.
  4. 4 Make methanol from wood. Place wood logs into a blast furnace (also connected to your dynamo with a cable). This cooks the wood and produces methanol, another liquid you need.
  5. 5 Mix them in the Backmix Reactor. Build a backmix reactor and connect it to your dynamo. Use fluid pipes with fluid extractors at each machine to pump the canola oil and methanol into the reactor. It mixes them together to start making fuel.
  6. 6 Separate the fuel in the Split Tank. Pipe the mixture from the reactor into a split tank. Over time (no power needed) it separates into two things: biodiesel — your actual car fuel — and glycerin, a leftover liquid.
  7. 7 Fill up your car. Pipe the biodiesel into a fuel station, then drive your car up to it and right-click to fill the tank. Now you're ready to go!
Tip Don't forget to place a fluid extractor on every machine you want to pump fluid out of — pipes alone won't move anything without one. Also use fluid filters (fill a bucket with the fluid you want, then right-click the pipe) so the right liquid goes to the right place.
Pro tip Once you have enough biodiesel, build a generator and run it on biodiesel instead of hand-cranking the dynamo. It's much faster and you won't have to stand there turning the crank!
